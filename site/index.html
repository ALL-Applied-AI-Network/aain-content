The problem

Your résumé looks like 243 others.

AI agents apply on students’ behalf now, so a posting that drew 116 applications in 2022 draws 244. They all read alike, and neither the recruiter nor the screening software can tell one from the next. What gets you pulled out of the pile is proof you built something real, and people who watched you do it.

How it works

Show up. Build. Get seen.

Every chapter runs the same four steps each semester. Walk into step one with zero experience. Open a step to see what came out of it.

Come hear from people who ship AI for a living. Every other week, free, open to anyone. NVIDIA, Direct Supply, Kohl’s and Generac have sent engineers to a room of forty to fifty.
