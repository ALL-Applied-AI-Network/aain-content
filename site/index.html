The problem

A résumé alone won’t get you hired.

AI résumé agents flood inboxes faster than you can apply. Applications per job posting more than doubled in three years, and they read alike. What’s left is the one thing a résumé can’t show: what you can build.

The formula

Show up. Build. Get seen.

Four steps, in order. Every chapter runs the same loop every semester. Free to join, and the first step asks nothing of you but showing up.

Show upTeamBuildGet seen
Step 01 · Show up

Come hear from real builders.

Speaker events with engineers who ship AI for a living. Free, open to anyone, no experience needed.

Step 02 · Team

Join a team project.

Zero experience is fine. Groups of five to eight with an upperclassman mentor, kicked off in week five.

Step 03 · Build

Solve a real sponsor’s problem.

Two to six months on a company’s actual problem. Cash, GPUs and publications on the line, even in your first semester.

Step 04 · Get seen

Get hired from the room.

Sponsors watch you build for months, then hire from the team they watched.
