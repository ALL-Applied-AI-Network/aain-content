For sponsors

Watch a team build on your problem. Then hire from the room.

An Innovation Lab puts a student team on a real problem from your company for eight weeks, with one of your engineers holding office hours once a week. You see how people work before you interview them. Direct Supply hired the majority of its 50-person AI team this way.

Before

NDA and IP, settled first.

During

Your engineer, one hour a week.

Office hours are what turn a hackathon into a lab. Students bring questions; your engineer sees who asks the good ones.

After

Code, judged, within two weeks.

Cleaned-up code handed over within two weeks of the showcase, three judges of your choosing, and a recommendation letter for every participant.

The money

Tax-deductible, no cut.

Your gift goes to a 501(c)(3), restricted to the chapter you pick. The Foundation holds it; the chapter spends it. We take nothing.
