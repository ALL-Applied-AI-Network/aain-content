Playbooks

Short guides to running a chapter, each a few minutes long. Every one starts with the three things to do first and names the dashboard tool for each job.

How they fit

One semester, two tracks.

A regular event every two weeks keeps people coming. A hands-on project, kicked off in week 5, gives them something to build. Each row is a playbook.
