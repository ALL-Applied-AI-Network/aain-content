Get sponsored

Help reaches you through the network.

You don’t cold-email companies alone. Three ways help gets to your chapter, and one rule for asking.

The ALL Network

  1. Ask the network

    File a request for a speaker, judges, food, prize money or a problem to work on. ALL’s team looks for the right company, and you see where each one stands.

  2. Find sponsors near you

    See companies near your school and where its alumni work, or check a list you paste. Request an intro, and ALL’s team reaches out for you. US chapters, once verified.

  3. Money, held the right way

    Gifts go to the ALL Applied AI Network Foundation, a 501(c)(3), restricted to your chapter. We take no cut, your chapter directs the spend, and the receipt states what’s tax-deductible.

The one rule

Ask for a speaker slot first, not money.

Most of the founding chapter’s sponsors were speakers first. It’s a smaller ask, and it gets them into the room. The Sponsorship playbook has the sequence and what to say in the first real meeting.

Questions

What officers ask first.

What does it cost?

Nothing. No dues for members, no fees for chapters, and ALL takes no cut of what sponsors give. Card processing on a gift is the only cost.

We already run a club. Do we start over?

No. Claim your chapter and keep your name. Bring your members in with Invite/Import Members: a sign-up QR, a spreadsheet, or another platform.

Do our members need AI experience?

No, and say so every time, because most students assume they do. Project teams mix newer students with one or two experienced ones.

Who holds sponsor money?

The ALL Applied AI Network Foundation, a 501(c)(3). Your chapter directs how it’s spent and no officer fronts or holds it. Money your university holds stays with the university and follows its rules.

What happens when we graduate?

Add next year’s board under Members → Officers & Roles. Events, the roster and the money history stay with the chapter, not with whoever set them up. Getting Started’s last chapter covers the handoff.

Is this only for US schools?

The dashboard, chapter site and playbooks work anywhere. Money, and finding sponsors through ALL, are for US chapters.
