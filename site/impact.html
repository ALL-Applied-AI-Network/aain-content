03 Network partnerships

Two ways to back the network.

Org-wide partners put compute, credits or mentors in front of every chapter at once. Chapter sponsors back one chapter directly. Either way the gift is tax-deductible, restricted to the chapters you pick, and we take no cut.

Org-wide partners

They power every chapter.

One partnership reaches every chapter at once: compute, API credits, mentors or tooling.

Chapter-specific sponsors

They fund a chapter directly.

A sponsorship through the portal funds one chapter, and every dollar lands in that chapter’s account. An Innovation Lab is $5,000; direct sponsorships start lower.
